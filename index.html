About Imperial Beauty Salon & Academy Patna

Established in 2021, Imperial Beauty Salon & Academy was founded with a vision to deliver luxury beauty, hair care, and HD bridal makeup services at Tamtam Paraw, Sadikpur, Patna City.

At Imperial Beauty Salon, we believe true beauty shines with confidence. Whether you need an advanced Keratin Treatment, Hair Botox, Nanoplastia, Hair Straightening, or a flawless HD Bridal Makeup for your special day, our certified stylists deliver perfection with extreme care.

From relaxing Hair Spa, Facials & Skin Rituals to Manicure, Pedicure, Waxing and everyday beauty essentials, we offer complete head-to-toe salon care under one roof in Patna City.

Alongside our salon services, Imperial Beauty Academy offers practical beautician training, and professional makeup & hair courses to help aspiring artists build a successful career.
