About Imperial

Imperial Beauty Salon & Academy is a professional salon and beauty academy dedicated to quality service, hygiene and career-focused learning.

Our salon provides a full range of beauty services including hair, skin and bridal makeup. Every client receives careful attention, professional advice and services using trusted products.

Our academy offers practical training in makeup, hair and beauty therapy for students who want to build a career in the beauty industry.
